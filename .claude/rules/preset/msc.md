# MSC, Motivated Software Concept

MSC is the author's lens for appreciating work and decisions across the ecosystem. Its definition and
the project's own profile live in one place, the project's MSC document named in `CLAUDE.md`; read it
rather than restating it.

- MSC is a concept, not a set of criteria. Never rank the six MSCs, and never call one foundational,
  heaviest, or strongest.
- When proposing or reviewing a design, describe where its attention goes for each MSC and whether
  that suits the project's profile. Do not score it or turn the reading into a verdict.
